Scientific Abstract & Contribution

The rapid proliferation of IoT edge devices introduces critical vulnerabilities during distributed intelligence workflows. Standard Federated Learning leaves model gradients susceptible to eavesdropping, parameter inversion, and poisoning attacks. This research presents an integrated post-quantum architecture utilizing Ekert 91 (E91) quantum entanglement to generate shared keys with eavesdropper detection via CHSH Bell violations. Coupled with authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption, the prototype maintains high FL model accuracy (85.0% F1-score) while enforcing zero information leakage across untrusted communication channels.

However, they cannot prevent an authenticated, legitimate insider from submitting an adversarial, poisoned model update. Robust aggregation provides the essential algorithmic defense layer at the parameter aggregation level.
